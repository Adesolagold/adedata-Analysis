# 📊 ALAB Sales Analysis Dashboard

A comprehensive Excel-based business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze retail sales performance, revenue generation, profitability, and regional sales metrics.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project processes raw transactional data to uncover key performance indicators (KPIs) for business growth. The interactive dashboard provides deep insights into product profitability, sales representative performance, geographical distribution, and monthly sales trends.

## 🛠️ Features & Visualizations Included

* **Interactive Filters (Slicers):** Filter data dynamically by Region (East, North, South) and Product Category (Electronics, Furniture, Home Appliances).
* **Product by Profit Analysis:** Bar charts highlighting top-earning products ranging from Sofa Classic to Air Conditioner X2.
* **City by COGS (Pie Chart):** Visual breakdown of operational costs across key markets including Abuja, Kano, Lagos, and Port Harcourt.
* **Sales Rep Performance:** Comparative bar charts evaluating revenue generation across team members (Aisha, Chidinma, David, Grace, Musa, and Peter).
* **Month of Performance Trend Line:** Tracks performance metrics across monthly intervals from January through May.
